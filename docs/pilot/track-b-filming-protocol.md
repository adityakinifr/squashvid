# Track B filming protocol: one instrumented session

**Purpose.** The pilot measures everything it can from the existing three
clips, but those clips have no ground truth: nobody knows where the ball
really hit the wall or where a foot really was. One session filmed with this
protocol gives us:

- the first measured accuracy for front-wall impacts, floor bounces and foot
  positions;
- a direct comparison of today's camera position with a higher tripod;
- a two-camera pair for testing triangulation;
- the court's true dimensions. Court 5 calibrates to **6.15 m wide** (the
  standard is 6.40 m) and about **9.94 m long** (the length is uncertain by
  ±0.1 m from video alone).

Total time on court: about 45 minutes, plus a feeder or a coach for the
targeted shots.

## What to bring

- Two phones with at least 30 GB free each, and a way to transfer **originals**:
  AirDrop, a cable or Google Drive. **Never send the session over WhatsApp**:
  it shrinks the video about 30× and deletes most of the ball.
- One tall tripod, or any mount that can hold a phone 2.5–3 m up, plus a phone
  clamp. A small tripod or clamp for the second phone, so it does not move.
- A 10 m tape measure.
- Painter's tape: blue on the white walls, white or yellow on the floor. Ask the
  club first, and remove it afterwards.
- A dark cloth or a rubber lens hood to press against the glass, which kills
  reflections.
- Free app: **Blackmagic Camera** (iOS and Android). The built-in camera app
  cannot set the shutter speed.

## Phone settings (both phones)

| Setting | Value | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Resolution / rate | 1080p at **60 fps** | Twice the samples of the ball's flight |
| Shutter | **1/1000 s** or faster (raise ISO to compensate) | Freezes the ball instead of a grey streak |
| Stabilisation | **Off** | It crops and warps each frame differently, which breaks the court geometry |
| Focus, exposure, white balance | **Locked** | Stops the picture pulsing when players pass |
| Lens | The widest lens that shows the **whole court**, as in today's clips | Both side walls, the front wall and the floor all the way to the front-wall junction must be visible |
| Codec | HEVC or H.264 at the app's high bitrate | Both are fine |

## Camera positions

- **Phone A (comparison).** Use the same place as today's clips: behind the
  back wall, chest height, looking through the door. Clamp it, don't hand-hold
  it. This tells us exactly what the old footage was missing.
- **Phone B (proposed).** Use a tripod 2.5–3 m high, centred behind the back
  wall, lens touching the glass with the cloth around it, tilted down so the
  front wall fills the top and the back of the service boxes is in frame.
- Take one photo of each phone's setup from the side.

## Before play: about 15 minutes

1. **Measure the court** and write the numbers into the table below. Measure
   to the edge of each painted line and say which edge.
2. **Tape targets.** Put 30 cm tape crosses at measured positions:
   - **front wall:** six crosses, at 0.8 m and 2.5 m high, at 1.0 m from the
     left corner, at the centre, and at 1.0 m from the right corner;
   - **floor:** six crosses, at 1 m from the front wall and 1 m from each side
     wall, on each service box's inner front corner, and in each back corner
     1 m from both walls.

   Measure each cross's centre from the **left side wall** (across) and from
   the **back wall** (along) for the floor, or from the left corner and the
   floor for the wall.
3. **Start recording on both phones, then clap once** where both can see and
   hear it. Do this again at the very end. The two claps let us synchronise
   the phones and measure any drift.
4. **Film 10 s of the empty court.**
5. **Foot ground truth.** One person stands still for 3 s each on the T, on
   each floor cross and on each service box corner, toes on the mark. Then
   they walk slowly along the short line, the half-court line and each
   side-wall junction.

## Targeted shots: about 15 minutes

A feeder hits each set and calls the shot name out loud; the audio becomes our
log. Aim at the tape crosses where relevant:

| Set | Count |
|---|---|
| Straight drives, each wall | 10 each |
| Cross-courts, each side | 10 each |
| Boasts, each side | 10 each |
| Drops to each front corner | 10 each |
| Lobs, each side | 5 each |
| Serves, each box | 10 each |

## Play: 10–15 minutes

Play normal rallies, or a game, with both phones running.

## Then

- Transfer **both originals** at full quality.
- Also send **one 1-minute piece of Phone A over WhatsApp**, so we can measure
  exactly what the compression destroys.
- Send the filled-in table and the two setup photos.
